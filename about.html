About

I'm a backend and platform engineering leader who has spent the last decade building systems that process money, inform financial decisions, and run at scale. My work spans everything from scrappy MVPs to compliance-certified multi-cloud platforms used by enterprises.

At BeyondIRR I grew the engineering team from 2 to 15, architected a multi-tenant portfolio management platform (10K+ clients, 9+ enterprise deployments on isolated AWS/Azure/Kubernetes stacks), and shipped a multi-agent AI system for financial insights using Pydantic AI, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL — connected over a reliable AGUI protocol. I also led ISO 27001 and DPDP compliance end-to-end.

My engineering instinct: systems should be simple at the boundaries and powerful at the core. I believe the best teams are built on clear ownership, honest code review, and the discipline to ship and observe rather than over-design upfront.

Outside work I run portfolio.ltd ↗ — a small design studio shipping privacy-first, offline-capable apps. I've been a 3× Google Summer of Code contributor and mentor for KDE, and I've spoken at FossAsia 2016 and QtCon 2016.
