About

I'm a backend and platform engineering leader who has spent the last decade building systems that process money, inform financial decisions and run at scale. My work spans everything from scrappy MVPs to compliance-certified multi-cloud platforms used by enterprises.

At BeyondIRR I grew the engineering team from 2 to 15, architected a multi-tenant portfolio management platform (10K+ clients, 9+ enterprise deployments on isolated AWS / Azure / Kubernetes stacks) and shipped a multi-agent AI system for financial insights using Pydantic AI, FastAPI and PostgreSQL. I also led ISO 27001 and DPDP compliance end to end.

Outside work I run portfolio.ltd ↗, a small studio shipping privacy-first, offline-capable apps and open-source developer tools. I've been a 3× Google Summer of Code contributor and mentor with KDE, and I've spoken at FossAsia 2016 and QtCon 2016.

I live in Hyderabad, India, and I'm currently open to engineering leadership, platform and applied-AI roles. The now page has what I'm up to this month, and the resume has the formal version.

How I work
01

Simple at the boundaries, powerful at the core

Interfaces should be boring and predictable. Complexity belongs inside well-owned modules, not leaking across teams through clever APIs.

02

Ship, observe, then design

Most upfront architecture is guesswork. Get a thin version into production with good telemetry and let real traffic tell you where the design needs to go.

03

Clear ownership beats consensus

Every service, pipeline and pager has one named owner. Shared ownership is how things fall between the cracks at 3AM.

04

Honest code review is a kindness

Review for judgment, not style. Say what you actually think, early, and expect the same back. It is the fastest way a team levels up.

05

Compliance is an engineering problem

ISO 27001 and DPDP went smoothly because controls were mapped to things engineers already do, with evidence generated by the pipeline rather than by hand.
